Biggest lessons from the Presentation Zen book

- Keep it simple
- There is only one message the audience will remember
- Don't put too many words on the slides
- Slides support the presentation, they are not the goal
- Practice  \n\n---\n\n Keep it simple

Avoid overcomplicating your slides and message, avoid clutter. \n\n---\n\n There is only one message the audience will remember

Most people int he audience don't care about your presentation. Think about the single most important thing you want to convey and make sure you make that message stick. \n\n---\n\n Don't put too many words on the slides

People are not good at reading and listening at the same time. Keep the text minimal - not more than 7 bullet points with not more than 7 words each. \n\n---\n\n Slides support the presentation, they are not the goal

The slides should support your presentation, not be self-contained. You don't need to put everything you say on the slides. \n\n---\n\n Practice

Practice your presentation many times. This helps me a lot as my brain has more capacity to look at the audience and see their reactions when I give a well rehearsed presentation. Helps with stage fever as well.
