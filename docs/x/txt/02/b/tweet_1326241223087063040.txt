Cognitive bias time - the IKEA effect 🛏️

People tend to place a unrealistically high value on things they helped create. 

Studies show that people would value IKEA furniture they assembled themselves 63% more than similar pre-assembled items!

There are real implications 👇  \n\n---\n\n Selling 💰

When we are selling things that we invested effort building or maintaining (like a car or a house), we tend to value it higher than the potential buyers. \n\n---\n\n Ideas 💡

We often think that ideas we have thought a lot about are brilliant, while they may not seem particularly interesting to others. \n\n---\n\n Working on projects 💻

Even worse, people (and especially managers) often continue pouring work and resources into projects they helped develop, even if much better alternatives become available.
