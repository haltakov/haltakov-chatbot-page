What I liked most was the discussion if lidar is needed for self-driving cars. As with many things in life, there is no universal truth here...

👇 Short thread 👇 \n\n---\n\n Waymo's Strategy

Waymo prioritizes safety for their  use-case - robotaxis. Their 5th gen cars have 5 lidars, along multiple cameras and radars. 

Their viewpoint is that why not use tech  that's available today, if it makes driving safer and more robust?

 \n\n---\n\n Tesla's Strategy

@elonmusk famously said that lidar is a crutch. Tesla is bringing autonomy to consumer vehicles where lidar comes with  trade-offs (cost, integration).

Tesla bet on the massive  amounts of video data they have  and improvements in CV.

 \n\n---\n\n Traditional OEMs Strategy

The traditional OEMs like Audi, BMW and Mercedes all announced using lidar for their L3-4 efforts.

They are not using the big 360° lidars like many other companies. They are going for smaller lidars integrated in the front, for increased redundancy.
