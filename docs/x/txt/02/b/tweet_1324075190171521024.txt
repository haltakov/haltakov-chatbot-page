Your software is crashing? Maybe you can blame it on the cosmic rays next time... 🤔

Cosmic rays are high-energy  protons or atomic nuclei  traveling trough space. They have enough energy to corrupt data if they hit an electronic circuit.

 \n\n---\n\n Unlikely to happen?  ☝️

A study from IBM in the 90s shows that computers typically experience  one cosmic-ray-induced error per 256 MB of RAM per month. This is probably even worse with the much smaller transistor sizes today.
