I've been thinking about the problem that new Twitter accounts have - tweeting and nobody seeing their tweets.

There are many small accounts that have very interesting things to say, but cannot spread their message.

I have an idea... 👇  \n\n---\n\n Idea 💡

A website that shows tweets anonymously. It would work like this:
▪️ Every day show a fixed selection of tweets (e.g. 100) without showing who tweeted
▪️ People read the tweets and vote for the good ones
▪️ On the next day, you see who wrote the tweets you voted for \n\n---\n\n Why anonymous? 🤔

I think this is key! The focus should be *only* on the content of the tweet/thread. It should also be stripped from external links, mentions etc. Just content!

Only on the next day when you see the original tweet, you can go and like it, retweet or follow. \n\n---\n\n Ranking 🔽

The votes can be used to rank the anonymous tweets, similarly to Hacker News, Reddit or Product Hunt. This may cause some problems with discoverability, though... \n\n---\n\n Submitting tweets ✉️

Adding a special hashtag to the tweet will "submit" it for display on the next day. All the tweets will be gathered and a selected subset will be shown. \n\n---\n\n Selection 🔍

This is the tricky part - how to choose the tweets to show. Here some ideas:
▪️ One account may submit only once per week (or month).
▪️ Accounts that got lots of upvotes previously get priority.
▪️ Accounts with low quality content get less priority.
▪️ Randomizer \n\n---\n\n What do you think❓
