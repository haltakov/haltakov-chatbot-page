I recently watched "The Social Dilemma" and even though I didn't like it a lot, it got me thinking about bubbles in social media. So I decided to do an experiment... 🥼🧪

Read below 👇 \n\n---\n\n The problem 🤔

The US elections were one of the very controversial topics recently. I saw that almost all people I follow support Biden, but a large number of people still voted for Trump. So it seems I built a nice, comfortable bubble with similar opinions as mine on Twitter... \n\n---\n\n The idea 💡

The other day I saw a fierce argument between somebody I follow and another person that was supporting Trump and was very vocal about it. I decided to follow that person and put them on my Prio 1 reading list, just to get a different perspective. \n\n---\n\n Result 📋

The experience has been mind-boggling! The person I followed often retweets information from other people that is mostly absurd. Mostly pro Trump, conspiracy theories and anti-mask... There is a whole world out there with facts so much different than mine... 🤯 \n\n---\n\n Now it is your turn 🎲

Try this! Pick somebody with very different opinions and believes than yours and who is active on Twitter and follow them. Maybe in all the wrong information you will find a grain of truth that will make you challenge some of your believes... \n\n---\n\n And many of the retweets are of people that have huge following - many with more than 200k followers and holding important positions in media and politics! \n\n---\n\n No it is your turn 🎲
