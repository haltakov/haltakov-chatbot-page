I like the Udacity nanodegree model:
✅ You get courses with very high quality  
✅ You work on interesting projects  close to the real world
✅  There are deadlines that push you to complete your projects
✅ You get guidance and career advice \n\n---\n\n Experience 👍

My wife is currently going through the Computer Vision nanodegree and it is really good! \n\n---\n\n Get a discount 💰

While the regular price of a nanodegree is quite high, Udacity constantly offers big discounts so keep your eyes open.

As of today, I could get a 40% discount and I guess students could get even more!  \n\n---\n\n Apply for a scholarship 📜

Check out of for company sponsored scholarships! This will allow you to get the program for free. You can also register and get informed as soon as a new relevant scholarship is launched.

 \n\n---\n\n Ask your employer to pay 💼

Udacity has a special program for employers that want to offer nanodegrees as a training.   Ask your employer and show them this page.
