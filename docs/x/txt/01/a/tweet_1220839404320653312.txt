Trick to save money on flights  – the “inverted return flight”! Replace two return flights from A to B, with one return flight from A to B and one return flight from B to A in-between.

Example of how I saved 20% on my last booking 👇 \n\n---\n\n [2/5] I want to book a return flight from Munich to Sofia in April and then again in August. Normally, you would book like this:

MUC-SOF from April 10 to April 19 (274 €)
MUC-SOF from August 8 to August 16 (344 €)
Total: 618 € \n\n---\n\n [3/5] Instead, you can book the same flights like this:

MUC-SOF from April 10 to August 16 (314 €)
SOF-MUC from April 19 to August 8 (200 €)
Total: 514 €

That’s 20% cheaper (105 €), for the exact same flights – same airline, same dates, same times! \n\n---\n\n [4/5] This works in cases, where one of the destinations is in a cheaper country than the other. In this case Bulgaria is much cheaper than Germany, so booking flights starting from Sofia is usually much cheaper. \n\n---\n\n [5/5] The example above contains real prices from Lufthansa from 2 days ago. I ended up booking slightly different dates, but I still used that trick and saved around 20%, on those otherwise really expensive flights...
