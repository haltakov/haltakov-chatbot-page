German health care system at its best today! My 1.5 yo had conjunctivitis today. We called 116 117 (24/7 number for non-emergency on duty doctors), a doctor called us back in 5 minutes and came by our house 15 minutes after that to take a look at him and prescribe a medicine.  \n\n---\n\n This was Easter Sunday morning and everything took less than 30 minutes in total and of course costs nothing... We didn't have to wait for after the holidays and we didn't have to go the doctor's office or a hospital. \n\n---\n\n My son had another eye infection the other day, this time during the week. We called his doctor's office, they asked for a photo of the eye and after an hour just faxed the prescription for eye drops directly to our pharmacy, which I then picked up. Cost 0€.
