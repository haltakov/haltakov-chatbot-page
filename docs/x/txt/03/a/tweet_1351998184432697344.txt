How to use Machine Learning in Finance? 🧠🤖💰

This is a true story that happened to me 7-8 years ago while working on my PhD. 

By chance, I was located at the same floor as one of the finance departments at the company.

One day one of the finance colleagues came for help...👇 \n\n---\n\n He was working on a big Excel spreadsheet full of finance data. The file grew with the time and got increasingly difficult to work with.

In the end, the file was more than 150MB so the colleague couldn't even open it anymore... \n\n---\n\n So, I let him sit on my workstation with dual Xeons and 100 GB RAM, which I used for training my ML models. He opened his file and exported the data he needed 🤷‍♂️

Machine Learning saved the day once again! 🏅 \n\n---\n\n Sorry, if you somehow expected more... 😂
