What is a 𝘁𝗲𝗻𝘀𝗼𝗿?

Tensor is used in TensorFlow and PyTorch to indicate a multidimensional array (like numpy.array). This may be confusing because the mathematical term actually has a different meaning - more like a function.

👇 \n\n---\n\n Tensors are data structures that are used to store data. For example:
▪️1D tensor could store prices of houses
▪️2D tensor could store an image
▪️3D tensor could store multiple images or a single image with 3 color channels
▪️4D tensor could store multiple color images
