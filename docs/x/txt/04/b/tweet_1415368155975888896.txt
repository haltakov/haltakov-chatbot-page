How it works - changing ads in the live stream of a football game ⚽ 📺 

I asked you yesterday how you would implement such a computer vision system and there were some quite interesting answers.



Let's see how it is done now 👇 \n\n---\n\n Magenta screen 🟣

The LED screens at the stadium show each frame for 20ms in total (50 frames per second), but 2ms are used to show a magenta color, while the other 18ms show the real ad.

This is too short for the people at the stadium to see, but not for the camera... 👇  \n\n---\n\n Cameras 🎥 

The cameras at the stadium are synchronized with the system so they can shoot a frame exactly in the 2ms the magenta "screen" is shown.

Replacing the magenta color with another video is a fairly standard technique in video production (chromakey).

There's more 👇 \n\n---\n\n Camera movement ↔️

The cameras are not static though - they pan, zoom and tilt. Aligning the ad video with the screen is not trivial.

The system uses additional tracking of the cameras to determine their orientation and improve the overlaying of the ad. \n\n---\n\n Performance ⏩ 

This approach is great, because it doesn't require lots of computation ressources.
