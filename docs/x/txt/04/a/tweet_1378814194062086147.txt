Are label errors a big problem in ML? 🤔

There is a new study from MIT that says, yes! They analyzed 10 popular research datasets (incl. ImageNet) and found 3.4% label errors on average in the test set (5.8% for Image net). This is a lot...



More 👇 \n\n---\n\n Even more interesting - small models that performed worse on the wrong label, were often better than the big models on the corrected data!

I've dealt with lots of labeling problems in the past and they can lead you to a completely wrong conclusion.

Make sure to fix your labels!
