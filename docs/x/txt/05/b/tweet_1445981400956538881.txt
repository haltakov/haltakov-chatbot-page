How fast can you label images? 🏎️

I'm labeling images of birds and not-birds for my machine learning course. My average velocity:

▪️2.4 images per second with a good tool
▪️3.2 images per second with a good pre-labeling pipeline

Pre-labeling made this task 33% faster/cheaper! \n\n---\n\n There is almost always a way to do some pre-labeling.

I have two stages:
1. User-generated tags on the photos
2. Running YOLO to detect birds

It makes it much easier to review photos of mostly birds and then photos of mostly not-birds.

 \n\n---\n\n I'm using a tool that Intel developed and open-sourced. It's called CVAT - 

It takes care of prefetching and caching. After that, it is one image at a time, but you can flip them very fast!

 \n\n---\n\n It is just a binary label bird or no-bird. CLIP is definitely a good idea as a kind of a better classifier than YOLO for this case.

My main point here is to show how a specific tool is used (CVAT) and YOLO is built in there.
