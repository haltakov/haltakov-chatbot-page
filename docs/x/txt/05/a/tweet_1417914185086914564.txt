Can the AI Wolf 🐺 catch the Sheep 🐑 ?

This is a simple game created by students in China. The goal is to train the 🐺 to catch the 🐑 in 20 seconds while staying away from the boulders (see rules).

The wolves started doing something strange, though... Can you guess what? 🤔  \n\n---\n\n More details 🗒️ 

The wolves have information about where the sheep are and how they are moving.

The simulation was run over 20 seconds and the scoring rules from the image above were applied.

The strategy of the wolves was trained in a reinforcement learning scheme. \n\n---\n\n Both @thCharon
 and @Kelson_SS
 are right! The best strategy for the Wolf face was to commit suicide as fast as possible in order to avoid losing 2 points just by wandering around without catching any Sheep.
