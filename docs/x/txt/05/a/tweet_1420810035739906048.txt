Imagine you can hit "undo" in real life...

You can instantly turn back time for whatever duration you want - 10 seconds, 1 day, 10 years.

However, the time you go back is deducted from your lifetime. You go back 1 year, you die 1 year earlier.

How would you spend it? \n\n---\n\n Yes, it would be very easy to make a lot of money on the stock market or with crypto! But would you use some of the other time?

 \n\n---\n\n Yes, this should be pretty easy! But will you use it for other things than money?

 \n\n---\n\n Yes, you keep your memories. But after 10 years you will again be 35... Will you then go back again and live your whole life at this age?

 \n\n---\n\n OK, let's say you fall down and get a really bad injury... Won't you go back 5 minutes and make sure it doesn't happen?

 \n\n---\n\n Good one! But if you die, you die - no coming back!
