Garbage in, garbage out... I don't like this phrase! 

I've seen a lot of cases where people use it as an excuse to be lazy and not put in the work.

Let me explain 👇  \n\n---\n\n In most real-world problems, and especially in ML and CV, we are dealing with imperfect data:

▪️There are typos in texts
▪️There are missing values in tables
▪️There is background noise in audio
▪️We try to recognize traffic signs that look like this

Shall we give up? No... 👇  \n\n---\n\n You need to put a lot of work to preprocess and clean up your data and make it usable for your algorithm. 

And often you can find that the garbage is actually not so bad and can help you solve your problem 😃 \n\n---\n\n Of course, there are some limits, and at some point, it really doesn't make sense to do anything with the data.

You should not give up too early, though!
