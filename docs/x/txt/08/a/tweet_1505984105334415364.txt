Weakly-supervised and self-supervised learning has huge potential in machine learning!

Just look at this paper - they are able to train a semantic segmentation model just from images and noisy text descriptions!


👇 \n\n---\n\n In general, semantic segmentation requires pixel-wise labels which are very expensive and difficult to obtain.

Sure, the results of the paper lack precision around the edges of the objects, but getting this quality just using text descriptions is very impressive!
