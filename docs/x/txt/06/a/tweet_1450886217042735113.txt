My little side project - AI interpretation of crypto art! 🐸

I use CLIP + VQGAN on an image from @cryptoadzNFT by @supergremplin and a heavily tweaked text prompt to create a cool-looking image and incorporate some interesting aspects.

▪️ Toad in space  \n\n---\n\n If you are interested in getting into this kind of AI-generated art, I recommend starting with this article by @sea_snell.
