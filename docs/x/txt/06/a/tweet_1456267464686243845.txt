If you liked my thread on AI-generated art, you'll like the Botto project by @quasimondo as well.



Botto is a Decentralized Autonomous Artist that creates AI-generated art. The interesting thing is that the community votes on the style Botto uses. \n\n---\n\n People can vote on different art pieces and styles using the Botto token. The votes are used to guide the prompt of the VQGAN+CLIP model. \n\n---\n\n Every Sunday Botto sells one of its images in an auction. The first two pieces were sold for more than $800,000!
