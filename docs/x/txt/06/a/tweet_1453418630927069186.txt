My review of the State of AI Report 2021

The State of AI is a fantastic report packed with interesting information, which is created once per year by the AI investors @nathanbenaich and @soundboy.



Here are the most interesting things for me 👇 \n\n---\n\n Predictions for 2021

They got 5.5 out of 8 predictions right

▪️ Huge NN models
▪️ Vision transformers
▪️ AI drug discovery company acquisition
▪️ AlphaFold 2 breakthrough
▪️ Nvidia an ARM  \n\n---\n\n Predictions for 2022

They'll probably get many of the predictions for 2022 right again (NIA 😄)
▪️Transformers for RL
▪️ASML $500B
▪️Anthropic 
▪️Consolidation in AI semiconductors 
▪️Small transformers reach SotA
▪️DeepMind breakthrough in physics
▪️JAX to 5%
▪️New AGI company  \n\n---\n\n Transformers

Transformers show great results problems outside of NLP
▪️ Computer vision
▪️ Speech recognition
▪️ 3D point classification
▪️ Chemical reaction prediction \n\n---\n\n AI  in Biology

Machine Learning is increasingly used in biology:
▪️ AlphaFold 2
▪️ Artificial proteins generation
▪️ 3D molecule structure prediction
▪️ Drug discovery
▪️ Chemical reaction prediction \n\n---\n\n Large models and zero-shot learning

Large language or multi-modal models (CLIP, DALL-E, GPT-3, Codex) show good zero-shot learning capabilities. The key is to ask the model in the right way - prompt engineering. \n\n---\n\n Graph Neural Networks

GNN are one of the new hot topics. They find application in:
▪️ Mesh-based simulation
▪️ Improving routing ETA predictions
▪️ Improving memory efficiency in large models
▪️ Reinforcement learning \n\n---\n\n JAX

A new machine learning framework introduced by Google in 2019 is gaining popularity. It is similar to numpy, but includes features like Autograd, vectorization, parallelization etc.

It is mainly used in research, but the community is growing fast and Google is pushing it. \n\n---\n\n Talent

▪️ China and India see big growth in AI talent
▪️ De-democratization - big tech companies and top universities have more access to computational resources and produce the most high-impact research.
▪️ Top academics going to big tech companies \n\n---\n\n There is also so much more interesting information in the report! You can check it out here:
