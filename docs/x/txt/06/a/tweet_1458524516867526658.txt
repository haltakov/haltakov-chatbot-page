Wen web3 Machine Learning community? 👀

Not the Web 2.0 ML communities on Reddit, Twitter, StackOverflow where contribution is rewarded with badges.

I mean a web3 community owned and governed by the members and one where all the value is distributed to them.

My vision (V1) 👇 \n\n---\n\n What do I want?

I want to be a part of a machine learning community where I can discuss interesting topics with like-minded people. Where I can learn new things, but also teach what I already know. Where I can help people solve a problem and get help for my problems.

But 👇 \n\n---\n\n I don't want a tech giant inserting ads and taking all the profits.

I want all benefits from the community to be distributed fairly to its members. I want the community to grow in value and to monetize it by offering info content, subscriptions, services...

OK, but how? 👇 \n\n---\n\n You can read my full rant from last week on the current state of Web 2.0 online communities, but today I want to focus on how to solve the problem!



Let's see 👇 \n\n---\n\n web3 Machine Learning Community DAO

A community where members:
▪️ Own a community crypto token
▪️ Discuss and vote how the community should develop
▪️ Receive tokens as rewards for contributions
▪️ Receive member-only benefits

Ideas on how to achieve this 👇 \n\n---\n\n ▪️ Crypto token

This is a typical way of organizing a Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO). Ownership of a part of the community is represented by a crypto token and is recorded on a blockchain. People can buy or sell the token on exchanges (DEX).

👇 \n\n---\n\n ▪️ Voting and governance

All decisions for the development of the community are made by the community members by voting. A member's vote has a weight proportional to how many tokens they own. Everybody can make a proposal and participate in the discussion.

👇 \n\n---\n\n In a DAO the community members have all the power to decide how the community should involve. 

What will be built? What services will be offered? How to monetize? Which contributions are more valuable?

The community decides and builds everything together!

👇 \n\n---\n\n ▪️ Tokens rewards for contributions

The value of a community is in the people. Members increase the value of the community with their work and are awarded tokens for that. 

What contributions are rewarded? 👇 \n\n---\n\n Contributors receive tokens for different things:

🏅 Building infrastructure (e.g. website, smart contract)
🏅 Teach ML
🏅 Explaining ML concepts
🏅 Helping people solve problems
🏅 Providing some service (reviewing a paper, writing code, consulting on a problem)
🏅 ...

👇 \n\n---\n\n Where do the tokens come from?

Initially from a token pool reserved for rewards to kick everything off. Possibilities for later:

📈 Sales of community created info content
📈 Subscriptions to exclusive content
📈 Ads on community property
📈 Other services
📈 ... 

👇 \n\n---\n\n 📈 Other services

I can imagine people coming to the community, asking for help and paying for it, like for example:

▪️ Find a solution to an ML problem (maybe even debugging code)
▪️ Getting help with training a model
▪️ Review of a research paper
▪️ Implement a method

👇 \n\n---\n\n 💳 Payment

The payment for a service can be either with the community token directly or with fiat. Fiat can be used to buy more tokens for the treasury, which is then used again to reward contributions.

This creates a circle where the tokens flow back to the community.

👇 \n\n---\n\n ▪️ Members-only benefits

Members get benefits for being a part of the community:
✅ Vote on decisions and shape the community
✅ Access to exclusive communication channels and events
✅ Provide a paid service
✅ Money when selling the token

Let me expand on the last one 👇 \n\n---\n\n ✅ Selling the token

As the community grows and gets more valuable, the value of the tokens members owns raises. Members will be able to sell some of their tokens and receive money.

But how does the community value grow? Let's see 👇 \n\n---\n\n I don't have all the details figured out, but I believe something like this needs to exist and with web3 we have the technology to go it.

There are already some similar projects like @developer_dao or @gitcoin, but not exactly what I imagine.
