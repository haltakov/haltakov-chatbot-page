(📜,📜), (🧱,🚀), (🐇,🎩), (3,3) - what are these symbols?

You've probably seen such symbols in people's names on Twitter, but do you know what they mean?

People use them to show they are belonging to a Decentralized Autonomous Organization or DAO.

Let me explain 👇 \n\n---\n\n DAOs

In short, DAOs are online organizations formed around a particular cause or governing a project. The DAO members may contribute time and resources, vote on governance decisions, and receive rewards, usually as crypto tokens.

But what about the symbols? 👇 \n\n---\n\n Many members are proud of belonging to the DAO so they are showing their support by adding the DAO symbols to their Twitter name.

The meme started with OlympusDAO and this image from their docs. In short, it describes the game theory behind the different actions DAO members can do with their tokens. Most beneficial for the community is if people stake their tokens giving a (3,3) reward.
