# Work at BMW

During my time at BMW I worked on several different projects:

- **Crowd-sourced camera based parking space detection** - I worked on the crowd-sourced parking space detection using camera images.
- **Computer Vision for Autonomous Driving and ADAS** - I worked on the computer vision pipeline for detection of traffic lights and traffic signs and some of the warning functions across 3 generations of production camera systems.
- **High-Precision Localization** - I worked on the high-precision localization of the vehicle in an HD map using varios sensors like GPS, cameras, Lidar and Radar.

The producsts I have worked on can be found in some of the production BMW cars produced since 2016 and almost all cars produced since 2018.
