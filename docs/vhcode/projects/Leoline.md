# Leoline

An AI assistant that tells stories to kids. Generate your story with Leoline [here](https://leoline.fun).

## Description

Leoline is a fun, interactive AI assistant designed for young children who can’t yet read or write. Kids simply talk to a cute orange rabbit, and Leoline creates and tells them a brand-new story based on their request.

## Background

My son loves fantasy stories and often asks for stories on specific topics—like _Mickey Mouse flying the biggest plane in the world_. He enjoyed using ChatGPT for stories, but its **voice mode wasn’t always easy for him**, and the stories were often **too short**.

So, I decided to create Leoline—an app specifically designed for kids. It’s **easy to use**, delivers **longer, engaging stories**, and ensures that content is **safe and age-appropriate**.

## Tech

Leoline is built with **TypeScript, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS**. It leverages various AI APIs for **speech recognition, speech generation, and content creation**. All assets are AI-generated.
