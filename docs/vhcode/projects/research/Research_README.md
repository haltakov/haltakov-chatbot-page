# Research Work

During my PhD I worked on semantic segmentation in camera and depth images and I focused on machine learning methods that incorporate context into the segmentation process. This was all before the era of Deep Learning.

You can find more details about my research on my [old university page](https://campar.in.tum.de/Main/VladimirHaltakov.html) and in my [PhD thesis](https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/node?id=1435905).
