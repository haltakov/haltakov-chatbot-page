question: What is Simple Muscle?
---

**Simple Muscle** is an AI workout tracker for iPhone that connects the assistant you already use to structured workout plans, logged sets, and training history.

The free core tracker works offline and includes unlimited workouts and exercises, rest timers, Live Activity, history, and charts. It records exercises, sets, reps, weight, duration, rest time, and completed sessions.

After you authorize a connection, ChatGPT, Claude, OpenClaw, and other MCP-compatible assistants can create or update plans and review your structured training history. The web dashboard and MCP connection are free; the optional iOS subscription adds cloud backup and continuous data sync.

Simple Muscle does not bundle another proprietary AI coach, sell workout data, or use it for advertising. It connects the assistant you choose, and that assistant only receives workout data when it requests it through an authorized connection.

You can learn more at [simplemuscle.ai](https://simplemuscle.ai).
