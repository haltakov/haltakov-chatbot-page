question: What is Simple Muscle?
---

**Simple Muscle** is an iPhone workout tracker that connects the AI assistant you already use to your workout plans, logged sets, and training history.

The app handles workout logging and rest timers. Compatible assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude can use the structured workout data through MCP to help create or update a plan.

You can learn more at [simplemuscle.ai](https://simplemuscle.ai).
