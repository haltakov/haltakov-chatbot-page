Here are some of the projects I usually point people to:

### [SimplePost](https://simplepost.social)

An AI-native social media scheduler that you connect to your own AI agent (ChatGPT, Claude, OpenClaw etc) so it can post and schedule. You can use the best AI model you are already paying for and posting at a much lower price than the others.

### [Simple Photo Gallery](https://simple.photo)

A tool for building beautiful, story-driven photo galleries. It started as an open-source CLI and grew into a modern hosted platform.

### [Chatbot Page](https://github.com/haltakov/chatbot-page)

A free and open-source library that allows you to easily create pages like that so your users can chat with your virtual self. It supports canned questions and answers (for lower cost) as well as full RAG and LLM model for more interesting questions.

### [Leoline](https://leoline.fun)

An AI storyteller for kids. Children talk to a cute orange rabbit, and Leoline creates safe, age-appropriate, longer-form stories.
