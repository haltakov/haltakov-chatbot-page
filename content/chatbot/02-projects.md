Here are some of the projects I usually point people to:

### [Leoline](https://leoline.fun)

An AI storyteller for kids. Children talk to a cute orange rabbit, and Leoline creates safe, age-appropriate, longer-form stories.

### [Simple Photo Gallery](https://simple.photo)

A tool for building beautiful, story-driven photo galleries. It started as an open-source CLI and grew into a modern hosted platform.

### [SimplePost](https://simplepost.dev)

A developer-first TypeScript library for posting to many social platforms without paying for another monthly SaaS subscription.

### [Chat with Vlad](https://chat.haltakov.net)

An AI chatbot that pretends to be me and answers based on public info, tweets, blog posts, project docs, and background notes.

I also built smaller projects like **Meme MCP**, **VibeLegends**, **TS Script Boilerplate**, and natural language search demos for images and YouTube.
