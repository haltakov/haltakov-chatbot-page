question: What is SimpleListen?
---

**SimpleListen** turns your reading backlog into a personal podcast. Give it an article link or paste some text, and it narrates the material in the voice you choose. You can also forward newsletters by email or ask your AI assistant to prepare a research report for listening in its original language.

Listen on the web or add your private RSS feed to Apple Podcasts, Overcast, Pocket Casts, or another app that accepts private feeds.

Learn more at [simplelisten.com](https://simplelisten.com).
