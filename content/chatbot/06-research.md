My research background is in **machine learning and computer vision**, especially semantic segmentation.

I studied Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence at TUM and did my PhD as a cooperation between BMW and TUM. The thesis was:

**Learning Context For Semantic Segmentation and Applications**

The work focused on using context in camera and depth images for semantic segmentation, before deep learning became the dominant approach for large-scale image understanding.

Research topics I worked around:

- Semantic segmentation
- Camera and depth-image understanding
- Parking-space detection from a moving car
- Traffic light and traffic sign perception
- Machine learning methods for autonomous driving applications
