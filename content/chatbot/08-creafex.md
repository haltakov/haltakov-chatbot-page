**Creafex Lab** is my company. I started it to build AI-powered products as a solo entrepreneur.

The idea is to integrate AI deeply into the whole process: product design, development, marketing, strategy, and internal assistants. I call some of these internal AI helpers **internobots**.

Projects connected to this phase include:

- **Leoline**, an AI storyteller for kids
- **Simple Photo Gallery**, a modern photo-story gallery builder
- **SimplePost**, a developer library for social media publishing
- Experiments around AI agents, MCP, and developer workflows
