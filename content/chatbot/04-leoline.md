question: Tell me about Leoline
---

**Leoline** is an AI voice assistant for kids, focused first on telling stories.

The motivation came from watching my son use regular voice assistants. They were not really designed for children:

- They interrupt too early when kids pause to think
- They often make stories too short
- They may use language that is too complex
- Parents do not get enough persistent control over topics, age, and style

Leoline tries to fix that with:

- Kid-friendly voice interaction
- Safe, age-appropriate language
- Persistent parental controls
- Longer stories generated in chapters
- A cute orange rabbit character that kids can talk to

Technically, it uses Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, speech recognition, story generation, voice output, and AI-generated/animated assets.
